This is a baby monitor app, with secure video and audio streaming, for Android and PC (Windows, macOS and Linux).
No accounts, no cloud servers, no telemetry, forever free and open source.

Nanna means "baby sleep" in Italian. But this is not just for babies!
Watch your pets, your plants, your home, or anything else you want to securely and privately keep an eye on.

Run the app in recording mode on an Android 8+ device or a PC with a webcam and microphone. Set another phone or computer to watching mode.
Devices must be paired once on the same local network: open pairing on the recording device, then scan its QR code or enter its
six-character code on the watching device. Connect anytime to watch and/or listen. Pairings can be removed from either device at any time.

Features:

* Secure video and audio streaming.
* Network discovery of recording apps on the same network.
* Secure pairing between devices, using a pairing code or QR code.
* Always upright video stream, regardless of how the camera is placed.
* Low light boost to see better in low light conditions.
* Silence detection to avoid sending audio when it's all quiet.
* Customizable recording quality.
* Draggable video feed to see the whole video feed on any screen.
* Keep streaming in the background or with the screen off.
* Mute audio and video independently from either device.
* Works on Android 8.0 and later.
* Supports multiple recording devices and multiple watching devices.
* Private local streaming, without accounts, cloud services or telemetry.
* Optional self-hosted relay for secure streaming between different networks.

The recording device shows a viewfinder, but only sends audio and video while a paired watching device is connected. Turn its screen off
to save battery. If audio or video is disabled on the recording device, watching devices cannot turn it back on remotely.

Nanna is also available as a desktop application for Linux, Windows and macOS, which can act as either a recording or a watching device;
it will work with the Android app in any combination. It is published together with this project's source code on GitHub.

An optional relay app lets paired devices reach each other from outside the home network. The relay only ever sees encrypted data, and you
host it yourself on a machine you control. Without it, the app streams only within a single local network. It is also published together
with this project's source code.

This app includes a crash reporting feature, where if the app detects a crash, it will ask you to directly email (or otherwise share) a
prepared crash report to the developer. This is completely optional, and no data is sent without your consent. Reports contain no
information about you.
